# M16 with Masterkey — playable prototype

Choose **M16 + Masterkey** under **Assault rifles** in a custom class and equip
it for your next spawn. The mounted shotgun is part of that gun, so it uses no
second inventory slot. **B** on keyboard or **D-pad down** with the updated
Peace controller config runs `weapalt`, MW2's alt toggle. Fire and reload use
their usual buttons. D-pad down replaces the old gesture binding.

## Switching, as in MW2

- **Y is MW2's weapon cycle.** From the Masterkey, Y is the M16. From the M16, Y
  is the next gun. So it is two presses from the Masterkey to the next gun, and
  Y never selects the Masterkey: coming back from the other gun, Y brings the
  rifle, whichever mode the gun was put away in (MW2's `CycleWeapPrimary`).
- **The mode switch is MW2's alt switch.** Either way it has no drop (MW2's
  `altDropTime` is 0) and a 730ms raise (`altRaiseTime`). `weapalt` is refused
  while a gun is going down and during that 730ms raise, as MW2's toggle is,
  and from the other gun it goes straight to the Masterkey as one ordinary swap.
  A sprinting player gets the ordinary drop and raise instead, as in MW2.
- **An empty gun switches itself, as in MW2.** With no shells left and none in
  reserve the Masterkey asks for its own M16 as its last shot leaves (or on the
  next pull of the trigger), and the M16 is up by the mode switch, no drop and
  the 730ms raise, once that shot is over, the pump still owed. An empty M16
  goes to the other gun, and the SPAS to the M16 in its rifle mode, but only
  if that gun has rounds in its magazine or reserve; a gun with a reserve
  reloads instead. With nowhere to go the gun stays and dry-fires again every
  500ms while the trigger is held (Peace plays no empty-fire sound for it;
  MW2 plays the gun's own). After the switch from the M16 or the SPAS (not
  from the Masterkey) Y is refused for 750ms, as in MW2, even when there was
  nowhere to go: a quick Y as the gun runs dry is lost and the switch goes on;
  `weapalt` is still taken (MW2's `CG_OutOfAmmoChange` and
  `PM_Weapon_CheckFiringAmmo`; `dev/tests/empty_switch_test.c`).
- **Times are MW2's** (`weapons/mp/m16_shotgun_mp`, `m16_shotgun_attach_mp`):
  the M16 drops in 449 and raises in 750; the Masterkey drops and raises in 829;
  its shot is 330 and its pump 430; its reload is a 1000 start that feeds the
  first shell at 600, 750 for each shell after it (in at 550) and a 1350 end.
- **The pump follows MW2's rules.** The shot ends READY on its last think and
  the pump starts from READY on the next, 430ms from there (MW2's
  `ShouldBeFiring`, then `CheckForRechamber`). A swap asked for during the
  330ms shot begins on the shot's last think and leaves the pump owed; so does
  one first asked for on the READY think after it, a whole drop either way.
  The pump's bolt is 1ms into it (MW2's `rechamberBoltTime` is 0, which MW2
  arms as 1), so it is taken on the pump's second think: a swap first asked
  for on that think is instant and leaves the pump owed (MW2's own
  frame-perfect swap), and one any later finds it paid and only cuts the
  picture short. An owed pump is the pump alone when the gun is back, 430ms
  from its first think: the shot is never played again.
- **The Masterkey fires once a pull** (`m16_shotgun_attach_mp` is Single Shot;
  `weapon.json`'s `fire_type`). Held through the shot, MW2's lock makes it
  READY on the shot's last think and the pump starts there: the bolt on the
  next think and the pump's end a think sooner than with the trigger let go
  (43 and 96 at 8ms, 22 and 48 at 16ms, 11 and 24 at 33ms, counting the shot's
  command as 0), and nothing fires until the trigger is let go; the earliest
  refire is the let-go trigger's. With the trigger held the instant swap with
  the pump owed is therefore on the think after the shot's last. The M16 is
  `fire_type` 3-Round Burst, as `m16_shotgun_mp` (below).
- **The Moni comes out of those rules.** Shoot, Y, Y, X, all inside the 330ms
  shot: the reload starts out of the shot, settles the owed pump on its first
  think, and the swap that was waiting finishes on that same think with no drop
  at all. It works on every timing inside the shot, needs both Ys (one Y only
  reaches the M16, whose switch has no drop to skip) and leaves the Masterkey
  owing its pump with no shell loaded. Nothing in the code names it, and the
  SPAS cannot do it: its bolt is not on the reload's first think. See
  `dev/tests/moni_test.c`.

## The rifle's burst, as in MW2

The rifle is MW2's `m16_shotgun_mp`: a 3-round burst (`weapon.json`'s
`fire_type` "3-Round Burst"), 64ms a shot. MW2 has no burst state; a burst is
its shot count, and everything below comes out of four things ported as they
are in the disassembly: the count, "a burst is pending" (the count above 0 and
below 3 with a round in the magazine, MW2's `BurstFirePending`), the pending
trigger pull (a fresh press of the trigger since the pull's first shot, MW2's
`UpdatePendingTriggerPull`) and the cooldown. Nothing in the code tests for the
M16: any gun given a burst fire type behaves this way.

- **A tap fires three.** Once the first shot has left the other two leave as
  each shot's 64ms runs out, the trigger let go or not. Counting the first
  shot's command as 0, they leave on 0, 8, 16 at 125 fps (8ms commands), on
  0, 4, 8 at 62 and on 0, 2, 4 at 30.
- **The cooldown.** When the last shot's time has run out the gun is ready but
  cannot fire for 200ms (MW2's `player_burstFireCooldown`), whatever the
  trigger does: from command 24, 12 or 6 until 49, 25 or 13.
- **The next burst needs a fresh press.** A trigger held since the first shot
  fires nothing more until it has been let go. A trigger let go and pressed
  again at any time after the first shot (inside the burst or in the
  cooldown) and still down when the cooldown ends fires the next burst on
  that very command: 49, 25 or 13 commands after the last one began, the
  fastest the rifle fires. A press let go again before then fires nothing;
  after the cooldown a press fires at once.
- **A burst cannot be interrupted by a button.** While a burst is pending MW2
  runs none of its weapon checks, so:
  - Y, the Masterkey (B) and a ladder wait: the swap's drop, the mode switch
    and the ladder's drop begin when the last shot's time has run out, in
    place of the cooldown (command 24, 12 or 6; `dev/LADDERS.md`).
  - A reload waits for the last shot and is then taken at once, out of the
    shot: R held from inside the burst reloads on command 17, 9 or 5, with no
    cooldown. A tap of R let go before the last shot has left is lost.
  - A knife or equipment press made inside the burst is lost, even if the
    button is still down afterwards. One pressed after the last shot has left
    is taken at once.
  - Sprinting starts (speed and meter) but the gun is lowered only once the
    burst is over.
  - Frozen (a round's countdown or end) the burst waits where it is and goes
    on when the freeze lifts. Death ends it.
- **A short magazine ends the burst.** With 2 rounds or 1 the burst stops with
  the magazine. On the command the dry shot's time runs out the cooldown
  starts and, on that same command: with rounds in reserve the reload begins,
  trigger up or down; with none and another gun to go to, the switch to it;
  with nothing anywhere the gun stays, and a held trigger dry-fires when the
  cooldown has run out and every 500ms after.
- **What comes out of it** (read from MW2's code, not watched in MW2; nothing
  in Peace names any of it):
  - YY inside the cooldown readies the rifle at once with its count still at
    3, so the next pull fires one round; held on, a whole burst follows that
    round's cooldown.
  - A Masterkey shell fired and the trigger kept down while B switches to the
    rifle: the rifle fires two rounds by itself when its 730ms raise is over,
    the trigger up or down (the shell counted as the burst's first shot).
  - A ladder grabbed with the trigger held since the burst's first shot: the
    drop hangs for as long as it is held, like a single-shot gun's, and the
    jump off, still holding, fires one round on its own command (the count 3
    to 4), then the cooldown, then nothing until the trigger is let go
    (`dev/LADDERS.md`, `ladder_test.c`'s `TestHeldTriggerBurst`).
  - The burstload (below).
- **The burstload.** Shoot, then X (`+usereload`) right after, let go before
  the third shot: a reload plays as the burst finishes, the server reloads
  nothing, and you can aim through it. It is the ordinary fake reload
  (`dev/FAKE_RELOAD.md`): while a burst is pending the reload request is
  neither read nor cleared, so the copy a snapshot brought your client
  outlives the burst while the server has already dropped its own. X still
  down on the third shot's command is a real reload as the burst ends; the
  plain reload key (`+reload`) never fakes.
- **Bots** let the trigger go between pulls, as a player must
  (`BotPullTrigger`), so they fire burst after burst and the pump and bolt
  guns shot after shot.

The rest of the rifle is `m16_shotgun_mp`'s too, from `weapon.json`:

- 40 damage out to 1500 units, falling in a straight line to 30 at 2000 and
  30 beyond (35 at 1750; MW2's `Bullet_GetDamage`), in every game type. Three
  rounds kill up close, four from 2000 on.
- 30 in the magazine and 60 in reserve at spawn (MW2's startAmmo 90 fills the
  magazine first); the reserve holds up to 180 (maxAmmo), which matching
  pickups fill.
- Reload 2029ms, the magazine in at 1100. First raise 850; every later raise
  750, drop 449.
- Sprint: 300 down, 300 up. Aim: 250 in, 250 out, zoom FOV 50.
- View kick per shot: pitch -30..60, yaw 45..-45, centre speed 1500, hip and
  aimed alike (`dev/VIEW_KICK.md`).

Still not MW2's, and why:

- **One reload length.** MW2's empty reload is 2359. Peace has one reload a
  gun; a second length must be chosen as the reload starts and remembered
  through it (one of the two `weaponAnimSeq` values left since the G19's
  selector widened it to 3 bits) and needs its own clip.
- **Hip spread.** MW2's (3 to 7, growing 0.6 a shot, 0 aimed) needs MW2's
  spread model, which is not ported; the rifle keeps Peace's (base 200).
- **Hit locations** (head 1.4), the **gun kick** and bullet penetration are
  not modelled for any gun.
- **Aim returns** 449ms before MW2's reload ends (`adsReloadTransTime`), and
  its zoom moves over part of the aim (`adsZoomInFrac`); Peace's aim stays out
  for the whole reload and zooms over all of it.
- **The magazine's last round**: MW2 may hold its shot for the fire
  animation's length (`fireAnimLength`, unknown); Peace's is 64ms like the
  others.
- `mag_out_ms` (300) is a sound cue of Peace's own; a kill is logged as the
  Machinegun's.

`dev/tests/burst_test.c` (in `run-masterkey`) drives all of the above through
the real `Pmove` at 8, 16 and 33ms commands, and the G19 beside it (its semi
and auto fire and its mode switch are what they were, with a pending pull
left over from the rifle and without); `ladder_test.c` the burst on a ladder
(`TestHeldTriggerBurst`, at 66ms too); `bullets_test.c` the damage through
the real server; `fake_reload_test.c` the burstload.

For a quick baseq3 test, enter `exec m16_test` in the console. It starts the
ladder map with this rifle and a SPAS, binds the mode toggle, and selects the
test loadout. It does not overwrite any saved custom class. Select your regular
class afterward to restore your next-spawn loadout.

The geometry and animations are original and basic, with reused game shot
sounds. The Masterkey's damage, ammo counts, sprint and aim times are still
provisional, and it can aim down its sights where MW2's cannot. Detailed
textures and polished hand animation are later tuning work. It is not an
imported MW2 asset.

- Rifle: 30 rounds, 60 in reserve at spawn (up to 180), magazine reload.
- Masterkey: 4 shells, 16 reserve, pump after a shot, individual shell loading.
- Fire during a shell reload stops it as MW2's does: at the end of the
  segment it is in, with that segment's shell, then the end plays. A press
  asks in the loop at once and in the start only once more than 0.4 of it has
  gone (an earlier press is forgotten); a trigger down as the start or a pass
  ends asks too. A press let go before the end still stops the reload, and
  queues no shot (`PM_AdvanceReloadSegments`; MW2's
  `PM_Weapon_CheckForReload`).
- Ammo, reload notes, pump debt and readiness remain in shared simulation. No
  special NAC detection or scheduled scope flash was added. The mode switch's
  0ms drop is MW2's own data for it, not a holster made free.
- Both ammo pools, the selected mode and pump debt follow the inventory instance
  through drops and pickups. Two copies have separate pools. Matching pickups
  scavenge reserves without refilling either magazine.

`assets/source/m16/weapon.json` owns the shared timing/ammo constants and animation
lengths, with the Masterkey's shot, pump and reload in MW2's own fields
(`fire_time_ms`, `rechamber_ms`, `rechamber_bolt_ms`, `reload_start_add_ms`).
`dev/tools/build_m16.py` generates the two IQM view variants, MD3 world
variants, sight/clip files, textures, icon, shader and `bg_m16_data.h` using the
Python standard library. Both variants show the attached shotgun; the active
mode places its muzzle tag on the correct barrel. The Masterkey has a weighted
pump, support hand and shell, and each variant has an `alt_raise` clip for the
mode switch: the trigger hand changes grips while the gun stays up. The
exporter writes the same bytes on every platform (it snaps the last-bit
differences between maths libraries to zero). Run it with `--check` for a read-only
freshness check, then `python dev/tests/m16_asset_test.py` for independent mesh,
rig, clip and transition validation.

`dev/tools/test-masterkey.ps1` boots an isolated local game and checks rifle fire,
the mode switch, pump and one-shell reload, with screenshots. Use
`-Renderer opengl1 -VmMode 2` to check the frame-only/QVM path too.
`test-m16-viewmodel.ps1` is a separate static art preview using `testgun`.

Protocol **89**, game **-18** added per-slot underbarrel ammo and dropped gun
metadata; protocol **90**, game **-19** carries the mode switch's kind of draw;
protocol **94**, game **-23** carries the burst's pending trigger pull
(`PWF_PENDING_PULL`, a fifth `weapFlags` bit, above the G19 selector's). Rebuild the executable and all modules together. IDs are appended:
M16/Masterkey are 18/19 in baseq3 and 21/22 in missionpack. Existing weapon and
saved-class IDs are preserved.
