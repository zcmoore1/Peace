## 1. Movement and camera

### Open

**Sprint**

- **Sprint on the think between the shot and the rechamber.** MW2's bolt gun goes FIRING, then one think in READY owing the action, then RECHAMBERING; `PM_Weapon_CheckForSprint` runs before `PM_Weapon_CheckForRechamber`, so a sprint on at that READY think raises SPRINT_RAISE and the action stays owed. Peace's one fire clip has no READY think: its shot ends on the think the fire clock reaches `WNOTE_BOLT_OPEN`, and step 1b lowers the gun on that think (`PM_ShotOver`) with the action owed, which step 4d then works whole. So the lowering starts one think sooner than MW2's, and a sprint begun on the think after the shot's last (MW2's READY think) already finds the action under way and waits for it. *Not modelled* (one think). MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578060, 0x575A30 (0x575B51-0x575B79), 0x576CC0 (0x576D64-0x576DA3), 0x577EE0 (wf15/research-boltsprint). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c` (`PM_ShotOver`, step 1b); `dev/tests/shotgun_reload_test.c` (`TestSprintOverTheCycle`, `SP_AFTER_OPEN`).

**Speeds and scales**

- **When sprint and aim are decided.** MW2's PmoveSingle decides aim (0x4BDDC0) and the sprint (0x56EE40) before the move, so the first aimed or sprinting think already moves at the new speed. Peace runs `PM_CheckSprint` and `PM_CheckADS` after the move, so that first think moves at the old speed: one think late, 8ms at 125fps (inferred effect). One more result: a crouch and a sprint pressed on one command from standing end crouched in MW2 (its sprint starts before `PM_CheckDuck` crouches, and the crouch bit ends it the next think) but standing and sprinting in Peace (`PM_CheckDuck` crouches first and the sprint then stands the player up, `stance_test.c` pins it). *Not modelled.* MW2: PmoveSingle 0x5743E0 (wf16/memo/MOVEMENT_PLAN.md section 1, Frame). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:4865-4866`.

**Stances, falls and the player box**

- **Stances and moves Peace lacks.** MW2 has lean (`leanf`, x0.4 speed; prone's bob factor 0.24 only while leaning), mantle (`PMF_MANTLE`), the last-stand crawl and the dive (`PMF_DIVING`, `perk_diveVelocity` 250). Peace has none of these (prone is MW2's: see below). `pm_flags` is full (18/18), so a new flag needs the wire format widened. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x406F80, 0x56D7B0, 0x4EB22E (re/bob_report.txt; re/res_re_landing.txt). Peace: `CLAUDE.md` (Capacity limits); `dev/STANCE.md`.
- **Dead box.** MW2 keeps the 70-tall standing box on the dead (its corpse is a separate entity). Peace keeps Quake 3's `DEAD_HEIGHT` (-8, a 16-tall box) for a dead player, while the eye falls at MW2's 180 a second to 8. *Owner choice* (decision D8, `dev/STANCE.md`). MW2: iw4x.exe 0x570B25, 0x570B5D. Peace: `code/game/bg_misc.c` (`BG_PlayerBox`).
- **Spectator box and eye.** MW2's spectator is a 16-unit box about the origin with the eye at 0. Peace's spectator keeps the standing box and eye. *Owner choice* (decision D8, `dev/STANCE.md`). MW2: iw4x.exe 0x570A9F-0x570AF0, 0x5706CC. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c` (`PM_CheckDuck`).
- **The respawned move.** While `PMF_RESPAWNED` holds (until a command with neither the trigger nor prone), MW2 keeps only the stance and trigger buttons and zeroes the move. Peace clears the flag on MW2's condition and ignores stance requests meanwhile, but still moves. *Not modelled.* MW2: PmoveSingle 0x574429, 0x574617. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c` (`PmoveSingle`).
- **Fall damage and landing stun.** MW2's fall damage runs from 0 at `bg_fallDamageMinHeight` 128 to 100 at `bg_fallDamageMaxHeight` 300 (soft surfaces separate). A damaging landing stuns: `pm_time` 35*dmg+500 (cap 2000), `PMF_TIME_HARDLANDING` (accel x0.25, friction control x0.3) and velocity x0.5..0.2. Peace keeps Quake 3's landing-speed thresholds (5 on `EV_FALL_MEDIUM`, 10 on `EV_FALL_FAR`; ducking doubles it, prone does not (MW2's landing reads no stance), water reduces it) with no stun; 9 Lives removes it. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x56FD10, 0x56FEF2, 0x5733E6 (re/res_re_landing.txt); dvars 0x7ADCF8/0x7ADC04. Peace: `CLAUDE.md:585`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1398-1436`; `code/game/g_active.c:567-578`.
- **Quake 3's landing timer** *(unsettled)*. Peace keeps Quake 3's `PMF_TIME_LAND` with a 250ms `pm_time` after a landing faster than 200 down, and Quake 3's slide-move rule that keeps the old velocity while any `pm_time` runs. The flag is written and cleared but never tested. MW2's `PM_CrashLand` sets `pm_time` only for a damaging landing's stun; its caller and its `PM_SlideMove` were not read for either rule. *Unverified.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x56FD10 (re/res_verify_landing.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1628-1634`; `code/game/bg_slidemove.c:230-233`.

**Ladders**

- **Ladder tuning.** MW2's ladder drops the gun to weapon 0 on `quickDropTime` (250ms), and its jump-off sets the flat velocity to `jump_ladderPushVel` along the view, reflected off the ladder when the view faces into it, with the x0.75 rise. Peace keeps a universal 100ms ladder holster (an owner call), adds its push along the face's normal (the same as MW2's only when looking straight at the ladder), and uses first-pass values for the climb 160, 4-unit reach, friction 5, accel 10 and auto-grab, since MW2's ladder move has not been decoded. The push speed (128), the rise and the ±100° look are MW2's (see "Ladder push, look and grab with an offhand out" below). *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578C72, Jump_PushOffLadder 0x56DA80 (re/res_verify_landing.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_public.h:212-216, 263-274`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1011-1020`; `dev/LADDERS.md:44-50, 84-88`.
- **Equipment on a ladder** *(unsettled)*. MW2's pmove offhand gate has no `PMF_LADDER` test. On a ladder the gun is already weapon 0, so a frag with no use hint goes straight to Prepare; whether the client sends the button there was not traced. Peace starts no knife or equipment while on a ladder; one already under way at the grab finishes first (MW2's ladder drop waits for it). *Unverified.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x579030 (re/res_verify_offhand-gate.txt). Peace: `dev/LADDERS.md:75-78`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3472-3482, 3516-3517`.

**Camera**

- **Landing dip.** MW2's landing event (0x70+surface) carries a dip of (h-12)/26*4+4, capped at 24 (3 while diving), and the gun dips a further 25%. Medium and light landings raise only footstep events. Peace raises a landing event on every jump (`ad88f5e`), but the dip is Quake 3's -8/-16/-24 from `EV_FALL_SHORT/MEDIUM/FAR`; the 150/300ms timing matches. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x4D3620, 0x56FDDF, 0x4DD07C, 0x59C7F0 (re/bob_report.txt; re/bob_verify.txt B16-B18). Peace: `code/cgame/cg_event.c:607-627`; `code/cgame/cg_view.c:395-401`.
- **Step smoothing.** MW2's cgame smooths camera z on steps with `cg_viewZSmoothingTime` 0.1s (max 16, min 1) and applies `cg_errordecay` before the bob. Peace uses Quake 3 step smoothing from `EV_STEP_*` events. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x596710, 0x5901E5-0x59035B (re/bob_verify.txt). Peace: `code/cgame/cg_view.c:300-310`.

## 2. Weapons and gunplay

### Open

- **Equipment roster.** MW2 has lethal frag, Semtex, throwing knife, Tactical Insertion, Claymore, C4 and Blast Shield; tactical flash, stun and smoke; and detonator offhands (`hasDetonator`, `OFFHAND_DETONATE`). Peace: `code/game/bg_loadout.c:322-327`; `dev/LOADOUTS.md:14-22`.

Peace: `code/game/g_missile.c:873-881`; `code/game/g_weapon.c:803-807`.
- **Throwback and Martyrdom.** MW2 lets you pick up an enemy frag near your feet and throw it back (a cook-out then credits the original thrower), and Martyrdom drops a live grenade on death. ## 5. Netcode and killcam

### Open

- **sv_clientSideBullets switch.** MW2's `sv_clientSideBullets` is a dvar (default 1) that can be turned off. Peace's bullets are always the clients', with no switch. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x2098DA8. Peace: `dev/SILENT_SHOT.md:207-208`; `code/game/bg_misc.c:1757`.
- **Other players' bullets.** MW2 seeds other players' shots from the client clock. Peace draws them from what the entity carries (snapped origin, whole-degree aim, `pos.trTime`) with no aim state, so with hip spread; it is exact only when the entity shows the shot's own think. *Not modelled.* MW2: CG_FireWeapon 0x4FB1D0. Peace: `CLAUDE.md:209-213`; `dev/SILENT_SHOT.md:176-179`.
- **ps.weapon during a quick action.** In MW2 `ps.weapon` stays the gun during an offhand; the viewmodel weapon is offHandIndex (`PWF_USING_OFFHAND`) and `es.weapon` stays the gun for opponents. In Peace `ps.weapon` becomes the knife or offhand; the fire event's parm names the gun, so opponents, the flash (`muzzleFlashGun`) and the melee kick still read the gun. *Not modelled.* MW2: BG_GetViewmodelWeaponIndex 0x4BE4D0, 0x56E936, 0x578E00. Peace: `dev/SILENT_SHOT.md:201-206`; `CLAUDE.md:716-723`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:2738-2753, 4416-4419`.
- **MW2 events Peace does not raise.** MW2 raises `EV_MELEE_SWIPE` on a knife press and `EV_FIRE_MELEE` at the hit, `EV_RESET_ADS` + `EV_RELOAD_START_NOTIFY` on every reload start, `EV_RESET_ADS` on every command sprint is held, `EV_RECHAMBER_WEAPON`, `EV_EJECT_BRASS`, `EV_USE_OFFHAND`, `EV_OFFHAND_END_NOTIFY` and `EV_EMPTY_OFFHAND`. Peace matches only the offhand press events and the fire event: reloads raise `EV_RELOAD`/`EV_WEAPON_NOTETRACK`, the knife hit is `EV_FIRE_WEAPON`, and `EV_RESET_ADS` comes only on the offhand press, so ring-overflow windows involving these events differ. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x5774E0, 0x5774A0, 0x575E60, 0x4BDDC0, 0x575A30, 0x577970, 0x577A10 (wf11/report-mw2-events.txt §2). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3198, 3221, 3536, 4419`.
- **Stance events in the ring** *(unsettled)*. MW2's stance events (its 6-9) can come every think (a blocked prone turn, a ladder with a crouch or prone bit, a stance not yet reset) and share the four predictable slots with the shot. Peace's server reads a command's last four events (`ClientEvents`), so a shot followed by four stance events in one command Pmove chops into five or more steps (pmove_fixed with a small pmove_msec, or a long command) deals no damage; with 66ms chops it never happens. Whether MW2's server can lose a shot the same way was not traced. *Unverified.* MW2: event table 0x795748, BG_AddPredictableEventToPlayerstate 0x4BD150. Peace: `code/game/bg_misc.c` (`BG_PlayerEventsToEntity`); `code/game/g_active.c` (`ClientEvents`); `dev/tests/stance_test.c` (`TestRingBudget`).
- **Lag compensation.** MW2 rewinds other clients by the shooter's latency for bullets and the throwing knife's flight (clamp 1000ms). Peace has no antilag: hits and knife flight trace current positions, so under MW2's clock moving targets need about 11ms more lead. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x4AACA0, 0x4C1120, 0x440040, 0x4EE5BE, 0x4399A8. Peace: `code/game/g_weapon.c:167-235`; `code/game/g_missile.c` (`G_RunMissile`); `dev/FAKE_RELOAD.md:136-138`.
- **Server frame.** MW2 runs a fixed 50ms server frame with one killcam archive sample per frame. Peace's `sv_fps` is variable (the shipped autoexec sets 20), the replay records each game frame with a 25ms floor, so at `sv_fps` 40 the silent-shot window halves; `cl_maxpackets` also varies (`snaps` no longer does: the snapshot rate is MW2's). *Peace-only.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x416FEF, 0x627257, 0x451D0A, SV_GetCachedSnapshot 0x4DC220. Peace: `dev/SILENT_SHOT.md:209-212`; `dev/FAKE_RELOAD.md:271-284`; `code/server/sv_snapshot.c:434`; `dev/peace-autoexec.cfg:34`.
- **Killcam.** MW2 shows a killcam after every death (2.5s, 4.25s for grenades, 5s when not respawning), and slows its final killcam down: `doFinalKillCamFX` ramps `setSlowMotion` from 1 to 0.25 over one real second from where the clip shows D-1000 (0.25 by D-375), holds it to D+500 and ramps back to 1 by D+1125, about 9.3s real for the 5.95s clip; MW2 also shifts the killer's archived playerstate by `psOffsetTime`. Peace has only S&D's round-winning killcam (or MW2's final one for the kill whose score ends a match), at game speed, with no `psOffsetTime` and no per-death killcam. *Not modelled.* MW2: maps/mp/gametypes/_killcam.gsc:39-67, 224-241; iw4x.exe ScrCmd_SetSlowMotion 0x5f5f50, Com_SetSlowMotion 0x446e20, Com_TimeScaleMsec 0x60b380, SpectatorClientEndFrame 0x5d7a90. Peace: `code/game/bg_public.h` (`KILLCAM_*`); `code/server/sv_snapshot.c` (`SV_ReplayStart`); `dev/SEARCH_AND_DESTROY.md` (Play).
- **IW4x trick scripts** *(unsettled)*. IW4x's fake-shot script presses X for one frame at 57fps and shoots two frames later (about 53ms); its wild-scope script presses knife and X on one frame during the return. Which order MW2 players use is unknown. Peace is measured at 57fps too: on your own host a shot two frames after X (34ms) is always a fake bullet and three frames after (51ms) 80% of the time; knife and X on one frame give a zoomload with no knife, and a fake reload then the knife gives the wild scope. *Unverified.* MW2: IW4x scripts; knife check 0x577670. ### Recently made to match MW2

- **Snapshot rate online.** MW2 replaces a client's `snaps` with a snapshot every 100ms when `onlinegame` is set (registered with default 1; IW4x's dedicated server sets it too), else 50ms, and sends a loopback or LAN client one every server frame whatever `snaps` says. Peace honoured `snaps` (default 20, 50ms) for every client, so its internet clients got snapshots twice as often as MW2's and their fake reload, fake bullet and wild scope windows were narrower than MW2 online (85fps, 50ms ping, `cl_maxpackets 30`: a shot 132ms after X was a fake bullet 8% of the time against MW2's 47%). Its server now applies MW2's rule (`sv_onlineGame`, default 1): that shot is a fake bullet 51% of the time (the harness's sweep of the same phases), and every ping row of the fake reload harness is an internet client at 100ms. Online, X now has to be held across one of those snapshots to be seen at all, as in MW2. Your own host and LAN are unchanged. MW2: SV_UserinfoChanged 0x401950 (0x401a21-0x401aa1), SV_SendMessageToClient 0x48fe90 (0x48ff84-0x49007d), `onlinegame` 0x404cfd; IW4x `Dedicated.cpp:67`. Peace: `code/server/sv_snapshot.c` (`SV_ClientSnapshotMsec`, `SV_SnapshotEveryFrame`, `SV_SnapshotDue`); `code/server/sv_client.c` (`SV_UserinfoChanged`); `code/server/sv_init.c`; `dev/FAKE_RELOAD.md` ("Online"); `dev/tests/replay_test.c` (`TestSnapshotRate`).
- **The host's snapshot, a frame late.** MW2's server runs in a thread of its own: `SV_Frame` only signals it and returns, so a host reads each snapshot in the next frame and predicts from a base one frame older. ioquake3's `SV_Frame` ran inline and its second event loop read the snapshot in the same frame. `Com_Frame` now holds what `SV_Frame` sends the local client until the next frame's first event loop; nothing else waits. Over every snapshot phase the own-host fake reload and fake bullet windows end exactly one frame later: the fake bullet at 85fps went from 36ms 68% / 48ms 44% / 60ms 20% to 92% / 68% / 44%. MW2: SV_Frame 0x627610 (signal 0x627695), server thread 0x627040 (packets 0x458160, G_RunFrame 0x415cd0, snapshots 0x4517b0), Com_Frame 0x47dd80, 0x47dfc6, 0x47dfe1; IW4x `Threading.cpp`. Peace: `code/qcommon/common.c` (`Com_Frame`); `code/qcommon/net_chan.c` (`NET_HoldLoopPackets`); `dev/FAKE_RELOAD.md` ("The host").
- **Client clock** (`a31954e`). `CL_AdjustTimeDelta` drifts the client to one snapshot interval plus 16ms behind the newest snapshot, only at timescale 1, and prediction starts from the time-selected nextSnap. ioquake3 aimed at the newest snapshot, so the fake shot used to need ping. Peace: `code/client/cl_timedelta.c:24-90`; `code/client/cl_cgame.c:836-905`.
