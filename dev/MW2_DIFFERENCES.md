## 1. Movement and camera

### Open

**Sprint**

- **Sprint over a shot or a pump.** MW2's sprint driver in `PM_Weapon` returns early while either hand is FIRING, RECHAMBERING or MELEE_*. The sprint goes on, and the gun enters SPRINT_RAISE only when that state ends, so a sprint never cuts a fire cycle or a rechamber. Peace's step 1b lowers into `WEAPON_SPRINT_IN` from any state but DROPPING/RAISING, FIRING and BOLTING included, so a sprint cancels the pump like a swap and the pump stays owed. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578060, 0x578074-0x578080 (re/res_verify_sprint-machine.txt; re/res_verify_sprint-in-air.txt:83). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:4022-4041`; `CLAUDE.md:129-138`.
- **Sprint from a crouch.** In MW2, crouch and prone are in the sprint end mask (0xCF35) but not the start mask (0xCC35). A toggled crouch can start a sprint: `PM_CheckDuck` stands the player up with `EV_STANCE_FORCE_STAND`, whose cgame handler resets the toggle, and a blocked stand refuses the start. In Peace, `PMF_DUCKED` both ends a sprint and refuses a start, so there is no sprint out of a crouch and no forced stand. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x56ED10, 0x56ED80, PM_CheckDuck, cgame 0x4DD48F (re/res_verify_sprint-machine.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3773-3777`.
- **Sprint speed and sprint strafe.** MW2 applies `player_sprintSpeedScale` 1.5 in the walk scale only, and scales `cmd.rightmove` by `player_sprintStrafeSpeedScale` 0.667 while sprinting. Peace applies a flat 1.4x on the ground only, with no strafe scale. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x7ADCA0, 0x572EFC, 0x573262 (re/res_verify_sprint-machine.txt; re/dvars.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:400-403, 1768`.

**Speeds and scales**

- **Base speed.** MW2 copies `g_speed` 190 into `ps->speed` every command. Peace keeps Quake 3's 320, and the autoexec does not override it, so walk, air and sprint all scale from 320 while jump height and gravity are MW2's. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x5E380A (push 0xBE 'g_speed'), 0x49366F (re/res_re_air-move.txt). Peace: `code/game/g_main.c:141`; `code/game/g_active.c:843`.
- **ADS walk scale.** MW2 scales the walk by 0.4 while `PMF_WALKING` (aim button with sight aiming, not reloading) or leaning (`PM_CmdScale_Walk`). Peace's `pm_adsScale` is 0.5, on the ground and in the air, though its comment calls it MW2's walking scale. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x572DB0, 0x56F470, 0x5726C0 (re/res_re_air-move.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:38, 404, 441`.
- **Crouch speed.** MW2's stance scale is 0.65 crouched (`player_duckedSpeedScale`) and 0.15 prone, lerped over 400ms on a stance change (`PM_CmdScaleForStance`). Peace uses Quake 3's `pm_duckScale` 0.25 with no lerp. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x572C50 (re/bob_report.txt, re/bob_verify.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:36, 1098-1099`.
- **Strafe and backpedal.** MW2 scales `rightmove` by `player_strafeSpeedScale` 0.8 and a backward `forwardmove` by `player_backSpeedScale` 0.7. Peace's isotropic Quake 3 `PM_CmdScale` strafes and backpedals at full speed. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x572DB0; dvars 0x7ADCFC, 0x7ADC44 (re/dvars.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:389-422`.
- **Ground friction and acceleration.** MW2 uses friction 5.5, stopspeed 100, and ground accel 9 standing, 12 crouched, 19 prone, x0.25 during a hard landing. Peace uses `pm_friction` 6 and `pm_accelerate` 10 for every stance and Quake 3's `pm_airaccelerate` 1; only stopspeed 100 and `PM_Accelerate`'s stopspeed floor are MW2's. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x56F040, 0x5733E6, consts 0x739B9C/0x6F6CBC/0x739B94 (re/res_verify_landing.txt; re/res_re_air-move.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:34-47, 341-350`.
- **Per-weapon move scales.** MW2 scales the walk by the weapon's `moveSpeedScale` and `adsMoveSpeedScale`, and the sprint meter by `sprintDurationScale` (WeaponDef+0x2F4/0x2F8/0x2FC). The four modelled guns have 1 for move and sprint duration, but `spas12_mp` has `adsMoveSpeedScale` 2 and `flash_grenade_mp` has `moveSpeedScale` 1.2. Peace has no per-weapon movement or sprint-time scale; only Expeditious scales movement. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x573850, sprint start 0x2FC read (re/bob_report.txt; re/res_verify_sprint-machine.txt); weapons/mp/*_mp. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:389-422, 3748-3822`; `code/game/bg_loadout.c:47-53`.
- **Damage slowdown.** MW2 slows the walk after a hit through the damage timer (`player_dmgtimer_maxTime` 750, `timePerPoint` 100, `minScale`). Peace has no movement penalty for damage. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe walk scale 0x572DB0 (re/res_re_air-move.txt §4); dvars `player_dmgtimer_*`. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:389-422`.

**Stances, falls and the player box**

- **Stances and moves Peace lacks.** MW2 has prone (stance scale 0.15, prone bob, the prone ADS rule), lean (`leanf`, x0.4 speed), mantle (`PMF_MANTLE`), the last-stand crawl and the dive (`PMF_DIVING`, `perk_diveVelocity` 250). Peace has none of these. `pm_flags` is full (16/16), so a new stance needs the wire format widened. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x572C50, 0x406F80, 0x56D7B0, 0x46D610 (re/bob_report.txt; re/res_re_landing.txt; re/res_re_ads-rule.txt). Peace: `CLAUDE.md:508-509, 665-666`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:899`.
- **Fall damage and landing stun.** MW2's fall damage runs from 0 at `bg_fallDamageMinHeight` 128 to 100 at `bg_fallDamageMaxHeight` 300 (soft surfaces separate). A damaging landing stuns: `pm_time` 35*dmg+500 (cap 2000), `PMF_TIME_HARDLANDING` (accel x0.25, friction control x0.3) and velocity x0.5..0.2. Peace keeps Quake 3's landing-speed thresholds (5 on `EV_FALL_MEDIUM`, 10 on `EV_FALL_FAR`; ducking doubles it, water reduces it) with no stun; 9 Lives removes it. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x56FD10, 0x56FEF2, 0x5733E6 (re/res_re_landing.txt); dvars 0x7ADCF8/0x7ADC04. Peace: `CLAUDE.md:511`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1309-1347`; `code/game/g_active.c:567-578`.
- **Player box and eye height.** MW2 puts the origin at the feet, with its own standing and crouch boxes and eye heights. Peace keeps the Quake 3 box: origin 24 above the feet, eye 26 above the origin, crouch eye 12. MW2-derived offsets, such as the death drop and the TI ground point, are translated by `MINS_Z`. *Placeholder.* MW2: none cited. Peace: `code/game/bg_public.h:50-54, 216-220`; `code/game/g_insertion.c:144-146`.

**Ladders**

- **Ladder tuning.** MW2's ladder drops the gun to weapon 0 on `quickDropTime` (250ms), caps yaw with `bg_ladder_yawcap` 100, and pushes 128 sideways on a ladder jump (`jump_ladderPushVel`) with the x0.75 rise. Peace's first-pass ladder uses its own values, "not measured MW2 constants": a universal 100ms holster, look clamped to ±90°, push 180, climb 160, 4-unit reach, friction 5, accel 10 and auto-grab. Only `BG_LADDER_JUMP_SCALE` 0.75 is MW2's. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578C72, Jump_PushOffLadder; dvars `bg_ladder_yawcap`, `jump_ladderPushVel` (re/dvars.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_public.h:188-191, 238-243`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:4515-4520`; `dev/LADDERS.md:18-24, 42-45, 68-72`.
- **Ladder grab with an offhand out.** MW2's ladder drop goes through `CheckForChangeWeapon`, which refuses every offhand state (0x10-0x15), so a grab does not take a pulled offhand away. Peace's grab clears the quick-action latch and takes the offhand with it, a cooking frag's fuse included. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578BC0 (refusal at 0x578BEA), 0x578C72 (re/melee_report.txt C14; re/offhand_report.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3359-3365`; `CLAUDE.md:391`; `dev/LOADOUTS.md:200-201`.
- **Equipment on a ladder** *(unsettled)*. MW2's pmove offhand gate has no `PMF_LADDER` test. On a ladder the gun is already weapon 0, so a frag with no use hint goes straight to Prepare; whether the client sends the button there was not traced. Peace blocks equipment while attached to a ladder. *Unverified.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x579030 (re/res_verify_offhand-gate.txt). Peace: `dev/LADDERS.md:61`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3359-3365`.

**Camera**

- **Field of view.** MW2's `cg_fov` defaults to 65 (max 80), with `cg_fovScale`. Peace's `cg_fov` is Quake 3's 90, horizontal at the screen's aspect. How each applies the value to the screen was not compared. *Placeholder.* MW2: dvar `cg_fov` 0x9FBE24 (re/dvars.txt). Peace: `code/cgame/cg_main.c:217`; `code/cgame/cg_view.c:455-508`.
- **Landing dip.** MW2's landing event (0x70+surface) carries a dip of (h-12)/26*4+4, capped at 24 (3 while diving), and the gun dips a further 25%. Medium and light landings raise only footstep events. Peace raises a landing event on every jump (`ad88f5e`), but the dip is Quake 3's -8/-16/-24 from `EV_FALL_SHORT/MEDIUM/FAR`; the 150/300ms timing matches. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x4D3620, 0x56FDDF, 0x4DD07C, 0x59C7F0 (re/bob_report.txt; re/bob_verify.txt B16-B18). Peace: `code/cgame/cg_event.c:607-627`; `code/cgame/cg_view.c:395-401`.
- **Step smoothing.** MW2's cgame smooths camera z on steps with `cg_viewZSmoothingTime` 0.1s (max 16, min 1) and applies `cg_errordecay` before the bob. Peace uses Quake 3 step smoothing from `EV_STEP_*` events. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x596710, 0x5901E5-0x59035B (re/bob_verify.txt). Peace: `code/cgame/cg_view.c:300-310`.
- **Pitch clamp.** MW2 clamps pitch only, at `player_view_pitch_up/down` 85°. Peace keeps Quake 3's clamp at ±16000 short units, about 88°. *Placeholder.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x5718D0; dvars 0x7A9F90/0x7AB3B4 (re/dvars.txt). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:4499-4510`.

## 2. Weapons and gunplay

### Open

**Roster and loadouts**

- **Roster and slot rules.** MW2 has dozens of guns by class, and the secondary slot takes only pistols, machine pistols, shotguns and launchers. Peace has nine catalog guns, and any two different guns can fill either slot. *Not modelled.* MW2: maps/mp/gametypes/_class.gsc. Peace: `code/game/bg_loadout.c:192-198, 264-268`.
- **Default classes.** MW2 has its own default classes. Peace's presets are Assault (MG + SPAS), Sniper (M300 + MG), Demolition (rocket + SPAS) and Support (plasma + lightning), each with frag and flash. *Placeholder.* MW2: maps/mp/gametypes/_class.gsc. Peace: `code/game/bg_loadout.c:10-15`.

**Fire cycle and reload**

- **Bolt-gun fire cycle (SPAS, M300).** MW2 fires (FIRING for fireTime), then rechambers in a separate RECHAMBERING state entered from READY at least one think later; the bolt closes at `rechamberBoltTime` with `EV_RECHAMBER_WEAPON` and `EV_EJECT_BRASS`. `spas12_mp` is 469 + 467ms (close 449ms in), about 936ms between shots; `cheytac_mp` is 50 + 865ms (close 600ms in). Peace has one fire clip with notes: SPAS 633ms (open 150, close 500) from 38 SMD frames at an inferred 60fps, M300 950ms (open 160, close 780) from Peace's own values. *Placeholder.* MW2: weapons/mp/spas12_mp, cheytac_mp; iw4x.exe 0x575A30, 0x5771C0. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1960-1968, 1997, 4326-4327`; `code/game/bg_m40a3_data.h:12-14`; `CLAUDE.md:756-759`.
- **SPAS Fayde result.** The MW2 SPAS Fayde fires at 51% of a plain refire. Peace's fires at 53% (344 of 648ms), which follows from the inferred 633ms fire cycle. *Unverified.* MW2: measurement cited in dev/FAYDE.md. Peace: `dev/FAYDE.md:41-45`.
- **SPAS-12 reload.** MW2's `spas12_mp`: reloadStartTime 1.0 with a shell at reloadStartAddTime 0.6 (reloadStartAdd 1), reloadTime 0.666 per shell, reloadEndTime 1.0. Peace: 283/417/533ms from SMD frame counts at an inferred 60fps (the pack has no .qc), the shell note at 250ms taken from CoD4 weapon files, and no shell in the start segment. *Placeholder.* MW2: weapons/mp/spas12_mp (not used). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:1914-1934, 2026-2028`; `CLAUDE.md:756-759`.
- **Empty vs partial reload.** MW2 has separate `reloadEmptyTime` and `reloadTime` (`m4_mp` 2359/2029, `cheytac_mp` 3867/2267). Peace has one reload per gun. *Not modelled.* MW2: weapons/mp/*_mp reloadEmptyTime. Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:2022-2050`.
- **Trigger interrupting a segmented reload.** MW2 interrupts on a fresh trigger press, in RELOADING, or in RELOAD_START only past 0.4 of it. Peace sends an interruptible reload to its END on a held trigger with no edge, in START or LOOP, as soon as a round is in the magazine. *Not modelled.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578670 (re/res_verify_reload-gate.txt §1). Peace: `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3164-3175`.
- **Firing on the reload's last command.** MW2 asks a reloading gun nothing, even on the command its reload ends, so a held trigger keeps a segmented reload looping. Peace lets the trigger fire on that command, so a held trigger and the reload alternate. *Owner choice.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578670. Peace: `CLAUDE.md:572-574`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:3931-3935`.
- **The M NAC.** MW2 allows a return to the gun while its pump is still workable. Peace does not implement it ("future work"). *Not modelled.* MW2: rechamberBoltTime; owner. Peace: `CLAUDE.md:158-159`.
- **Bolt cancel on the close note's own think** *(unsettled)*. MW2's bolt close is a weaponDelay that expires in the per-hand step after the change check, and `CheckForChangeWeapon` accepts RECHAMBERING (578C13-578C2F), but its same-think ordering against a swap, sprint or ladder grab has not been read. Peace's rule: a swap, sprint or ladder grab completing on the close note's own think is instant but leaves the whole pump or bolt owed; one think later the chambering is kept. *Owner choice.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578BC0, 0x575A30, 0x47F620 (not traced for this case). Peace: `CLAUDE.md:129-147`; `dev/SPAS12.md:38-40`.

**Swaps**

- **YY cancels an ordinary raise.** MW2's YY during a drop readies the gun at once (states 3-5 only); by the binary reading, a raise with time left is not interrupted, so YY does not skip a draw (inferred). Peace lets YY cancel an ordinary raise ("not a bug"), but not a quick action's recovery raise. *Owner choice.* MW2: iw4x.exe 0x578BC0, 0x578C13-0x578C31, 0x578D0E-0x578D36 (re/melee_report.txt C17; sl/review3.txt). Peace: `CLAUDE.md:433-436`; `code/game/bg_pmove.c:2084`; `dev/SPAS12.md:7`.

**Damage and health**

- **Damage and range falloff.** MW2 gives each weapon damage/minDamage over maxDamageRange/minDamageRange (`m4_mp` 30 to 20, `spas12_mp` 40 to 20 per pellet, `cheytac_mp` 70). ## 5. Netcode and killcam

### Open
